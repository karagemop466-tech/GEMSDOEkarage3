Controls for the proxy

What chance looks like on the same proxy

Before any candidate is compared with the focus band, its score must beat a random-placement null. The null places the same 37,654 binary dots uniformly in the footprint (known-fault pixels excluded) and is scored by the same evaluator.

0

Random null

Five random draws score 0.0693–0.0732 (mean 0.0710) on the off-catalogue SGMC proxy, with per-draw 95% CIs in evidence/control-random-null-sgmc.json. S6-E1 (0.0452) is below that. S6-E2 (0.0990) is about 0.03 above it, but its CI lower bound (0.0750) sits just above the top of the null range (0.0732).

Focus band

The nine focus files span 0.0943–0.1024 on the same proxy, so the focus band sits about 0.023–0.031 above the null mean.

2

Leakage and mimic checks

S6-E2 uses one LiDAR-derived feature. Its canary AUC is 0.709 against the SGMC truth (threshold 0.90). That passes, but it is high enough that the proxy may reward LiDAR-visible structure in general. Road control: 8.25% of S6-E2 dots lie within 100 m of a mapped road, against 16.20% of the footprint.

Why GEMSDOE32 led the nine

Mechanism, checked pixel by pixel

1

Nested, not independent

Exact pixel tests: GEMSDOE32 ⊂ GEMSDOE31 = True; GEMSDOE31 ⊂ GEMSDOE25 = True; GEMSDOE32 ⊂ GEMSDOE36 = True; GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical dot masks = True. GEMSDOE32 is GEMSDOE31 with 2,545 dots removed.

2

The removed dots sit on known faults

All 2,545 dots removed from GEMSDOE31 to make GEMSDOE32 lie within 2.0 px of a visible catalogue fault (labels.tif). Official scoring masks known faults (DrivenData staff, 16 Sep). Removing a dot lowers FP_w by that dot's own non-negative term. It can also raise FN_w if the dot was the nearest hit for an unmasked truth point within 300 m, and hidden labels cannot be checked locally. The reported step 0.2708 → 0.2778 is consistent with a net gain; it is not verified.

3

Fewest dots of the nine

GEMSDOE32 has 37,654 dots, the lowest of the nine (next lowest: GEMSDOE28 at 37,660). Removing dots can only reduce FP_w if the removed dots carried no TP_w, and the reported ordering is consistent with that. Recall at the official metric is not computable locally.

Why the uniqueness rule blocks every dot file

The calibration lattice covers the footprint

A

206,895 lattice dots

Registry raster r13-lattice-s5 (scored/13gems_20261001_r13-lattice-s5_v2_nan-outside.tif, SHA-256 b4570b406efe…). It lies within 3 px of 99.87% of the footprint. Only 6,649 footprint pixels lie outside its reach.

B

Pigeonhole bound

Each hole pixel can hold at most one dot, so at most 6,649 dots of any N-dot file can fall outside the lattice's reach. Overlap is therefore at least 1 − 6,649/N, which exceeds 70% once N ≥ 22,164. Every focus file overlaps the lattice by 99.9% or more.

C

Consequence

Under the literal rule you set (no exemptions, including dense or synthetic lattices) no dot file of this size can pass. We have not waived the rule, and this page does not propose a way around it.
