2. The new file (H1): generated, unique against the other eight sites, blocked by the lattice

Method (pre-registered, fixed before evaluation): H1 magnetic+gravity horizontal-gradient ridge, off-catalogue, NMS, 20k. Layers: tmi_hg (magnetic horizontal gradient) and iso_grav_anom_hg (gravity horizontal gradient), both from training_features.tif. A dot is kept only where both edge maps are high (score = min of the two robust z-scores), at local maxima of a 7×7 window, with dots within 3 px of a catalogue fault removed, and a 20,000-dot budget (upper bound).

Result: 14,186 dots (below the 20,000 budget; the budget is an upper bound). Leakage canary: every feature alone scored AUC 0.45–0.56 against the catalogue, so no leakage was detected. Largest overlap vs the nine site files: 18.43%. Overlap vs 13GEMSDOE lattice: 99.45% (limit 70%) → BLOCKED.
