Why GEMSDOE32 (0.2778) led — and how karage3-unique-offcat aims to beat it

The GEMSDOE32 submission (h33-h33-2-b2, reported 0.2778) is a pruned subset of the GEMSDOE31 family: 2,545 dots near known catalogue faults were removed. The distance-weighted Tversky metric (α = 0.2, β = 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) penalizes false positives and rewards recovered hidden truth. Removing redundant collar dots cuts the FP penalty at near-zero true-positive loss, lifting the reported score from 0.2600 to 0.2778.

1

Prune dots near known faults

GEMSDOE32 removed 2,545 dots within 200 m of existing_faults.tif. Those dots were largely false positives — near catalogue traces but not at hidden truth locations.

2

Target off-catalogue faults

Hidden geothermal vents are not all in the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue. Dots placed away from known traces (off-catalogue) have higher true-positive yield per emitted pixel.

3

Stay under the uniqueness floor

A registered 206,895-dot lattice covers 99.87% of the footprint within 3 px. Any submission needs ≤22,163 dots for a theoretical overlap ≤70%. karage3-unique-offcat uses 12,000 dots in cells >3 px from every existing focus-site submission.
