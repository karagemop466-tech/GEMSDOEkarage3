Official competition & science sources

Problem & metric ↗

DrivenData describes the task, data, distance-weighted Tversky calculation and submitted raster format.

Mask clarification ↗

DrivenData staff says the known-fault mask is pixel-exact; neighboring pixels are scored normally.

Official rules ↗

Rules describe weekly feedback limits, final selection, eligibility and generative-AI disclosure. A deadline-time discrepancy should be clarified with the organizer.

USGS GeoDAWN ↗

Airborne magnetic and radiometric data release. The official page states CC0 and describes GeoTIFF and survey products; binaries were not downloaded here.

USGS 3DEP ↗ / 3DHP ↗

Elevation and hydrography sources. USGS describes 3DEP as free and unrestricted; target tiles and 3DHP coverage/license remain unverified locally.

DOE GDR INGENIOUS ↗

Public regional compilation of faults, geophysics and geothermal observations. GDR states CC BY 4.0; the listed external ZIPs were not downloaded in this runtime.

Southern Gabbs Valley study ↗

A documented blind geothermal case combining structure, geophysics and temperature. It does not validate this project’s candidate ideas.

What was actually restored

The shared, pinned GEMSDOE32 restorer fetched four core owner-mirrored rasters and checked their SHA-256 hashes. The large files live in an ignored cache, not in Git. The derived feature stack and official-authenticated competition files are not established.

The mirrored sample contains the same positive pixels as the mirrored labels, while the official description calls the example a total-absence prediction. This inconsistency is unresolved; use the mirror only as a geometry/provenance aid until confirmed.
