What must change before anything is cleared

  1. Owner decision on dense surfaces. 59 failing rasters have at least 1,000,000 positive cells, so their "dots" cover the whole footprint and no dot file can be under 70%. The rule needs a definition for continuous surfaces, for example top-K cells by value, before those rasters can be compared fairly.

A selector would need to put each candidate dot where, for every registry raster, at most 70% of dots fall within 3 px. That is a constrained selection and was not run here.

Hypothesis, mechanism and mimic (for the record)

Hypothesis. H-LG1: LiDAR step_max ordering of off-catalogue covered cells, combined with all lattice-uncovered cells, gives positive HOLDOUT-DTI on USGS SGMC off-catalogue faults.

Mechanism. A fault scarp is a vertical step in the 1 m DEM; cells with larger step_max are more likely to sit on a scarp.

Named non-fault mimic. Road cut-banks, berms, terrace or fan edges, and DEM/lidar processing artifacts raise step_max without faulting.

Leakage canary. Single feature step_max: AUC vs SGMC off-catalogue truth = 0.568 (threshold 0.90): pass (below threshold). Feature chosen after viewing single-band AUCs against the same SGMC truth; the SGMC number is therefore optimistic.

Holdout (HOLDOUT-DTI). GEMSDOE32-HIDE-RECOVER-v3.0: pooled DTI 0.0000, CI [0.0000, 0.0000], n=22,232. Zero by construction: every dot is >3 px from the catalogue; this evaluator scores withheld catalogue segments.
