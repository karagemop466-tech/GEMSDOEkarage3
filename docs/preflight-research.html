ARCHIVED 27 OCTOBER HYPOTHESIS SLATE

Earlier ideas, preserved for comparison.

These prior proposals overlap existing lines of research; global novelty and feasibility are unverified. Read the current three-candidate slate, official data-access checks and blockers → · Archived background slate and controls

01   Paired signed gravity–magnetic profiles Medium cost

Orient cross-sections through gravity, TMI/RTP and detrended terrain; compare signed multiscale changes and offsets to seek a concealed discontinuity. Lithologic contacts, remanence and survey leveling can mimic it. Related edge approaches exist in sibling sites. The provided feature stack is currently absent.

02   Cross-fan drainage-offset consistency High cost

Use USGS 3DEP terrain to match multiple channels across a persistent break in slope. A coherent offset may indicate a subdued fault; fan avulsion, stream capture and roads can imitate it. Tile coverage and actual binary access remain unverified.

03   Depth-differential conductance reversal Medium / high cost

Compare gradients in USGS MT conductance layers at different depths with gravity margins. Clay, saline fluids and inversion resolution are alternatives. The official source lists files, but their local bytes and suitability for trace-scale mapping remain unverified.

04   Cross-line residual thermal gradient High cost

Correct well/spring temperatures for depth and sampling density before testing a residual contrast across independent lineaments. Uneven sampling, lithology and anthropogenic wells are serious confounders. The public source lists a downloadable archive; binary access and full provenance remain unchecked.

B2 FORENSICS

Why might the historical file have scored well?

On hash-checked public mirrors, GEMSDOE32 is exactly GEMSDOE31 after removing 2,545 dots near mapped faults. Under a distance-weighted Tversky metric, such a deletion can help if the reduction in false-positive cost outweighs lost truth coverage. Hidden truth and score-to-file receipts are unavailable, so causation and any improvement beyond the USER-REPORTED 0.2778 remain unknown.

The competition asks for faults indicative of geothermal resources—not confirmed vents. A lineament, a model probability, and a geothermal discovery are different claims.
