## 1. Scientific Motivation: Why Missing Hidden Faults Differ Physically from `existing_faults.tif`

In the Northwestern Great Basin / Walker Lane transition (GeoDAWN survey footprint, EPSG:32611, `3,730 × 3,292` cells at `100 m` resolution, `5,167,373` valid footprint cells), the public training compilation (`existing_faults.tif` / `labels.tif`, `60,988` positive cells) already incorporates the USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database (`QFaults v2`: `59,064 / 59,065` cells lie within `300 m` of `labels.tif`).

Peer-reviewed structural and geothermal syntheses of the Great Basin — Curewitz & Karson (1997), Coolbaugh et al. (2006), Faulds & Hinz (2015), Faulds et al. (2016, 2021 — DOE INGENIOUS Play Fairway, DOI `10.15121/1881483`), and Siler et al. (2019, 2023 — USGS Structural Discontinuities) — establish two fundamental facts:

1. **Catalogue Completeness Bias (`e(x) << 1`):** Long (`> 10 km`), simple, high-throw piedmont range-front normal fault scarps are nearly `100%` mapped in `existing_faults.tif` — and their mature clay-rich fault cores are frequently **hydraulically sealed** against geothermal upflow. Conversely, **`~75%` of Great Basin geothermal systems are "blind"** (lacking mapped Quaternary surface scarps or boiling surface vents) because they sit on:
   - **Distributed Riedel ($R, R'$), relay-ramp breach, and horsetail termination splays** where tectonic throw is partitioned across multiple short (`1–5 km`), low-offset (`< 0.3 m`) strands whose subtle scarps are erased by Holocene alluvial fan aggradation or Pleistocene Lake Lahontan (`~1,335 m`) wave planation; and
   - **Intra-basin buried horst/graben steps and volcanic upland transfer zones** where `1 m` 3DEP LiDAR is either absent (`24.63%` footprint gap) or subdued by erosion and sediment cover.
2. **Supervised Positive-Unlabeled Suppression (`IR-PU-FARFIELD-01`):** Any classifier trained naively on `s(x) = existing_faults.tif` learns $P(s=1 \mid x) = P(y=1 \mid x)\,e(x)$, suppressing true unmapped faults in low-observability alluvial basins and upland interiors where `e(x)` is small.
