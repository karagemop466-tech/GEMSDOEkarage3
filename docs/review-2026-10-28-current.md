## What the 0.2778 result does and does not tell us

The supplied score for `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` is **USER-REPORTED**, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED here: no submission receipt ties that number to saved bytes and a raster hash. The parallel-source geometry record in [`knowledge/focus9_forensics.md`](../knowledge/focus9_forensics.md) reports that the champion mask is the GEMSDOE31 “solo” mask minus 2,545 points within a 2-pixel catalogue collar. It also reports the surrounding owner-supplied sequence `0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778` as thinning and then near-catalogue pruning are applied.

That sequence supports a plausible metric-based mechanism, not a proven causal account. The repository's metric implementation uses a 300 m triangular kernel and DTI weights α=0.2 for false-positive mass and β=0.8 for false-negative mass (`src/karage3/metric.py`). Clustering many closely spaced points can repeatedly cover already-covered target neighborhoods while still adding false-positive burden; thinning and removing predictions near known traces may reduce redundant/likely masked predictions. The hidden test labels are unavailable, so we cannot determine which deleted pixels were false positives or true positives, and a leaderboard score alone does not identify the optimal mechanism. Nor does fault propensity establish geothermal vent occurrence.

The highest listed focus-site score is therefore not enough to assert that a model can beat it. In the previous H-K3C work, the historical result was **HOLDOUT-DTI** (`GEMSDOE32-HIDE-RECOVER-v3.1-24cell`, 66,904 withheld positives): 0.111044 [0.098990, 0.122727] for the E1 construction rule, versus a same-evaluator champion-*rule* value of 0.100798 [0.090492, 0.110863], paired Δ +0.010103 [0.003019, 0.017272]. These are holdout numbers, not an estimate of the live score; the evaluator does not reproduce the reported 0.2778 on the champion's construction-censored mask. The live-score projection in the old card is explicitly a projection and is not used here as a score estimate.
