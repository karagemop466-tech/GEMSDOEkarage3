Why GEMSDOE32 reached 0.2778 (analysis)

  1. It is not a new structure. Its dots are a subset of the GEMSDOE31 family. Exact-pixel subset check: GEMSDOE25 (100.0% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE30 (100.0% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE31 (100.0% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE36 (100.0% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE33 (84.0% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE28 (69.7% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE24 (67.8% of its dots are exact pixels), GEMSDOE27 (67.8% of its dots are exact pixels), CAND-k4-offcat-ridge-20k (0.3% of its dots are exact pixels), CAND-geoconduit-hybrid (0.1% of its dots are exact pixels), CAND-unique-offcat (earlier) (0.0% of its dots are exact pixels). The chain of pruned catalogue-adjacent dots is what the repo's earlier review reported (detail); the subset numbers above are our own check.
  2. The reported ranking tracks off-catalogue skill, not catalogue recovery. Spearman(USER-REPORTED, SGMC-cal) = 0.950 and Spearman(USER-REPORTED, HOLDOUT-DTI) = -0.849 across the nine sites (n=9; scores USER-REPORTED, not organizer-confirmed). GEMSDOE32 has SGMC-calibrated 0.0641, within 0.0003 of the top of the nine (GEMSDOE36 0.0644). On the hide-and-recover HOLDOUT-DTI it is the lowest-tier file (0.0045), because it prunes dots that sit on catalogue faults.
  3. Why that pays on the portal (hypothesis, not proven): if the hidden test positives are off-catalogue faults, removing dots that duplicate catalogue traces frees budget for off-catalogue placements, and the score rises. The 9-point correlation supports this; it does not prove it.

  • Literal scan irregularities. (a) The scan's JSON keeps only the first 50 failures; all 108 failing rasters are in evidence/k4-literal-uniqueness-scan-rows.jsonl. (b) Three GEMSDOE23 rasters have undefined Pearson rho (NaN, constant raster), which the script treats as passing the correlation stage; they fail on overlap anyway. (c) Several overlap failures come from dense binary masks (over one million 1-valued pixels), where 3-px overlap is near 1.0 by construction. The gate is applied as written and not relaxed for these. (d) Eight rasters are grid-mismatched and skipped; one is unreadable.
  • Unit tests: 105 run, 4 fail on the untouched base commit as well (stale page assertions for an older csst-bghf tree and the H-GEO-CONDUIT card). Not fixed here; they are not caused by this session's changes.
