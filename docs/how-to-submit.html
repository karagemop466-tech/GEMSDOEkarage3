Before a future upload

  1. Confirm approval in the project. The current run card must have a positive verdict, pass the literal uniqueness checks against a complete current registry both before placement and on final dots, and record an independent selector approval. A local format check alone is not approval.
  2. Download only the exact approved artifact. Use the filename linked from the current executive summary. Verify its SHA-256 against the selector card; do not substitute an older run, ZIP, or similarly named file.
  3. Re-run the saved-byte validator. The official problem description requires a single-layer 32-bit float GeoTIFF, EPSG:32611, the training grid resolution/bounds, predicted values in [0,1], and null or NaN outside bounds. This repository additionally checks exact shape/transform, masks, and all in-footprint values. Do not infer organizer acceptance from a local pass.
  4. Open the official competition page. Sign in to the DrivenData DOE GEMS competition and open its submission form. Its data and account quota require the authorized account session; this repository cannot confirm your current remaining slots.
  5. Select the approved file and enter its short note. Use the exact unique name/note from the run card. Keep the note within 140 characters and avoid claiming an unverified geological discovery or score.
  6. Save the organizer receipt. Record the submission ID, timestamp, exact file hash, note and returned score. Only a receipt-backed result may be labeled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. A public leaderboard rank is not a receipt for your file.

Official format and sources

Read the official problem description, metric and submission format and the competition rules. The competition predicts faults indicative of geothermal resources; a fault prediction is not proof of a geothermal vent.

The portal previously returned “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”. The value-range condition is explicit in the official format. This guide does not claim to know the specific cause of the earlier rejection; only the portal receipt or organizer support can confirm it.
