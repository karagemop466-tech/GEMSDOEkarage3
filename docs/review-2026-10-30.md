## Literal-lattice constraint (geometry diagnostic, not a score)

The reproducible check `scripts/verify_lattice_bound.py` records 5,167,373 footprint cells, 206,895 lattice points, and 6,649 cells farther than 3 pixels from any lattice point. Thus the minimum possible overlap for a support of size N is at least `(N - 6,649) / N`, provided the support is inside the footprint. A 37,654-point mask has a lower bound of **82.34%**, and a 44,090-point mask **84.92%**; neither can satisfy the project's literal ≤70% rule. The largest support not ruled out by this witness alone is 22,163 cells. This is a pigeonhole bound against one registered calibration raster, not proof against the full registry or evidence about geology. Spending the remaining uncovered ring to evade the bound is explicitly refused by the project protocol as boundary gaming. Evidence: `evidence/lattice-bound-20261030.json`.
