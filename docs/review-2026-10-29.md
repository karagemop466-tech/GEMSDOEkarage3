Removing predictions near already-known faults can reduce false-positive burden under a distance-weighted Tversky-style score if nearby predictions already receive kernel credit or are masked, while preserving useful coverage elsewhere. This is a plausible metric mechanism, **not causal proof**: hidden truth, exact organizer receipts and artifact-to-score binding are unavailable. It does not establish that a new model can exceed 0.2778. A holdout proxy is not a leaderboard estimate.
