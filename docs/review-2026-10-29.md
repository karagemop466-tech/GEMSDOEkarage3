## Why the GEMSDOE32 B2 file may have scored better — and what is not known

The reported 0.2778 is not organizer-confirmed in this repository. The pinned pixel audit establishes **support lineage**, not why a hidden-label result occurred: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have the same in-footprint support; GEMSDOE32 is a subset of GEMSDOE31 after 2,545 catalogue-near pixels are removed; GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 dots to GEMSDOE32. Under a distance-weighted Tversky-style objective, pruning predictions that do not add much nearby truth coverage can reduce false-positive mass while sacrificing little recall. That is a plausible metric-level explanation, not causal evidence about hidden faults. The triangular kernel decays with distance; it does not award full credit across an entire 300 m disk. The raster geometry does not establish that any predicted line is a geothermal vent.

Whether a *new* model can outperform the historical claim is unknown. It has not been demonstrated by a protocol-valid, matched HOLDOUT-DTI comparison in this checkout; there is no valid mapping from the available holdout claims to a hidden leaderboard score. The correct score estimate remains **NOT ESTIMABLE**.
