What the pixels establish

An independent parallel audit downloaded nine selected leaders plus an earlier GEMSDOE28 solo base for research only and verified their pins. Our additional audit checked nine historical masks and the registered lattice against the mirrored grid.

  • GEMSDOE28’s earlier solo base and GEMSDOE31 each have 40,199 positive cells and the same in-footprint positive pattern. This is not the later 37,660-dot GEMSDOE28 selected leader.
  • GEMSDOE32 has 37,654 dots: exactly 2,545 base pixels within two grid cells of the mirrored catalogue were removed; no dots were added.
  • GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive-pixel masks despite different file hashes and outside encodings. GEMSDOE36 contains all GEMSDOE32 dots and 1,200 extra.

These are RASTER-AUDIT measurements, not HOLDOUT-DTI or organizer scores. Exact pattern equality is within an owner-mirrored proxy footprint; the mirror itself is not authenticated as the original organizer template. See the ten-artifact audit, the parallel nine-raster audit, and the newer priority/lattice audit.

A plausible metric explanation

The official metric gives distance-tolerant credit and weights false negatives more heavily than false positives. Dense predictions can spend mass covering the same neighborhood. Removing low-credit or redundant dots may improve the tradeoff if fault coverage is retained. But real catalogue-adjacent undiscovered faults may also be lost; there is no hidden ground truth or controlled ablation here to determine the effect of these removals.

Why none is a new eligible submission

Every selected historical file has more than 70% of its dots within three pixels of another audited file. The independently reproduced lattice bound rules out the familiar 37,654-dot budget under the literal any-registry rule. These partial comparisons prove specific failures, but do not certify a complete registry pass or check continuous surfaces. Some historical files passed a shared all-finite format audit against a hash-pinned owner mirror, which does not prove the template’s authenticity or acceptance by the organizer.

Can we exceed the reported result?

Possibly, but no new model was tested here and no numerical projection is supported. The current research slate has changed: magnetic crest migration already exists in prior upstream work. The ranked directions explain alternative proposals, their mimics and remaining novelty uncertainty. The core mirror and shared 35-channel stack were restored in this session, but the assigned lane, scientific evidence, complete registry and probability-safe shared contract are still missing.
