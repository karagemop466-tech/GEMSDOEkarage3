Automatic candidate gate decision · Prerequisite inventory
  1. Measure scientific performance. Require versioned HOLDOUT-DTI, withheld-positive count, spatial 95% CI, feature-only leakage canaries and paired improvement over the same-evaluator current best on identical folds. A projection is not a score.
  2. Establish actual novelty. Compare the pre-placement surface and final dots against every registry raster. Reject rank correlation above 0.90 or more than 70% of dots within three pixels of one reference. A new file hash, metadata edit or compression setting is not enough.
  3. Check raw predictions before writing. Reject NaN/Inf, negatives and values above one. Use the shared template writer, but never rely on its sanitization to conceal an upstream error. The inspected writer/evaluator APIs binarize positive inputs; do not mistake them for probability-preserving APIs.
  4. Reopen the saved TIFF. The shared validator and hash-bound conservative diagnostic must check the actual bytes: one band, finite values, [0,1] range, and exact CRS, shape and affine transform against the supplied template. An all-finite zero-outside file may avoid a whole-array NaN range failure, but its compliance with the official outside-null wording is not independently verified. Do not assume either zero or NaN encoding will be accepted. The ten historical artifacts were checked this way; that does not certify a new model or authenticate the mirrored template with the organizer.
  5. Publish only after gates pass. Show the new method-based filename, one-click TIFF download, SHA256, unique submission name and note ≤140 characters at the beginning of the site. No new candidate exists in this run, so no candidate name or misleading download is offered.
  6. Keep slot selection separate. The selector must confirm eligibility, current competition rules and weekly cap from the submission page. Neither a format pass nor a scientific “promote” alone grants upload authorization. Local testing uses no submission slot.
  7. Submit and save the receipt. After selector authorization, choose the single-band TIFF on the competition form, paste its short note and retain the organizer receipt linked to that file’s SHA256. Only a receipt establishes ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED performance.
