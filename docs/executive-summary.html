THE PREVIOUS RANGE ERROR

Inspect values, not just the extension.

Automated diagnostics inspect the saved file for one float32 band, exact grid/CRS/transform, finite in-footprint values, and range [0, 1]. They also distinguish whole-array finiteness from null-outside encoding.

The official null-outside wording and the reported portal range error can conflict with naive NaN or zero encoding. Local checks do not prove the portal will accept a file; no current candidate has been tested there.
