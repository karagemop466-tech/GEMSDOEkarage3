What was tested

Hypothesis. Uncatalogued faults are the missing pieces of catalogued systems: tip continuations, parallel splays and linkage zones. A learner trained on simulated catalogue incompleteness, with visible-catalogue geometry as input, should recover them.

The catalogue geometry roughly doubled holdout DTI: mechanism Δ +0.0733 [+0.0605, +0.0859]. The leakage canary passed (max single-feature AUC 0.788).

Why it still fails. The holdout withholds catalogue fragments that sit next to visible traces, so they are easy to recover. Live new faults are depleted near the catalogue. Under the live catalogue the learner concentrates its dots in the catalogue halo, which is already occupied by registered rasters. This is the duplicate stop. Fixing the holdout comes first (see the research note, §4 and §8).

Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778 (OWNER-REPORTED)
