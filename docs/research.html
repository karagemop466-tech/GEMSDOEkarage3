Understand the target

The lattice counting proof

In the owner-mirrored footprint, the registered lattice’s three-pixel neighborhood covers all but 6,649 cells. For any candidate containing B distinct in-footprint dots, at least max(0, B − 6,649) must be in the covered set. At B = 37,654, the lower bound is 31,005 / 37,654 = 82.34%, exceeding the 70% stop. This is geometry, not a score; it does not rule out every smaller budget or establish a full registry pass.

Do not relocate dots into holes to game the test. A calibration-grid exception would require an explicit protocol change; it is not silently assumed. Open the exact comparisons and inputs →
