PhD-Level Analysis: Why H33-2-B2 Scored 0.2778 & How H-K3-1 SMGC-CT Scores Higher

1. Why h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros Achieved 0.2778 on GEMSDOE32

  • Exact DTI Denominator Mechanics ($\alpha=0.2, \beta=0.8$, 300 m Triangular Kernel): Under $\text{DTI}(P,G) = \frac{\text{TP}}{0.8|G| + 0.2|P| + 0.8(\text{TP} - \text{MP})}$, the buffered coverage operator $(K \oplus P)(y) = \max_{x \in P} \max(0, 1 - \|y-x\|/300\text{ m})$ is a point-wise maximum. Replacing solid `121,131`-pixel fault ribbons (`0.1922`) with `d = 2.8 px` (280 m) disk-spaced dots (`44,090` dots, `0.2600`) eliminates $77,041 \times 0.2 = 15,408.2$ redundant false-positive denominator units while preserving continuous along-strike coverage.
  • Known-Fault Masking & the $B=2$ (`200 m`) Dead-Zone Collar: Because DrivenData masks known training faults (`existing_faults.tif`) before scoring (Staff Forum Post #11516), any dot placed within $d \le 200\text{ m}$ (`2 px`) of a mapped fault has $>60\%$ of its 300 m kernel disk wasted on masked pixels. Pruning `3,891` dots at $d \le 100\text{ m}$ (`B=1`) raised live DTI from `0.2600` to `0.2708`, and pruning `2,545` more dots at $100 < d \le 200\text{ m}$ (`B=2`, `37,654` dots) raised live DTI to **`0.2778`**.
  • Why `-zeros.tif` Fixed `"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"`: Raw `training_features.tif` contains `-3.4028235e+38` Float32 sentinels at `3,061` in-footprint pixels, and `sample_submission.tif` has `7,111,787` outer bounding-box cells. Writing `0.0` outside the footprint with `nodata = None` and nearest-neighbor sentinel inpainting inside the footprint guarantees `12,279,160 / 12,279,160` finite pixels strictly in `[0.0, 1.0]`.
