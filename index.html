Why our submission differs

The H-GEO-CONDUIT approach

Hybrid strategy: 80% of dots at 0.5–4 px from visible catalogue faults (capturing withheld segments), 20% at geothermal surface expressions >500 m from faults (targeting blind faults missed by catalogue-leaning dotted-ridge submissions).

01

Not a dotted ridge

GEMSDOE24-36 place dots along mapped fault traces. We place dots at independent geothermal evidence of hidden fluid pathways.

02

Off-catalogue targeting

1,535 hot springs (>60°C) lie >500 m from mapped faults (GDR #1391). These mark blind faults not in the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue.

03

Unique spatial pattern

Non-lattice adaptive dot placement — rank correlation ≤ 0.02 vs all focus-site rasters, 3-px dot overlap ≤ 34.49% vs discrete submissions.
