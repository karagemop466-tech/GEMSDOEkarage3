Why our submission differs

The H1 stepover-jog damage-zone approach

H1 targets blind geothermal-conduit faults missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue by placing dots in the damage-zone annulus 500 m–1.5 km from catalogue traces, at high local fault density (the stepover/jog proxy). GEMSDOE24–36 and H-GEO-CONDUIT cluster dots 0–2 px from mapped traces and prune within 2 px; H1 deliberately occupies the annulus they vacate, so it targets blind upflow beside — not on — catalogue traces, with zero dots on any catalogue pixel.

01

Distinct spatial family

H1 dots sit 5–15 px off catalogue traces; measured vs the nearest local registration: 0% of dots within 3 px, rank correlation −0.003 — well inside the lane rule (≤0.90 correlation, ≤70% within 3 px).

02

Blind-fault targeting

Stepover/jog damage zones are dilational conduits where geothermal fluids ascend without a separately mapped fault trace — precisely the catalogue gap the dotted-ridge family does not address.

03

No leakage

All dots are off-catalogue (0 on labels.tif), so the submission cannot be credited to known faults and carries no catalogue leakage.
