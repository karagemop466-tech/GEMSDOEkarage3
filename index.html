Submission gates — automated, fail-closed

1 · scientific test

Hide and recover

Hold out whole fault segments with a buffer. Recompute catalogue-based features from visible faults only, mask them pixel-exactly, and report pooled HOLDOUT-DTI with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% interval.

2 · leakage and uniqueness

Compare before and after placement

Test each feature alone for leakage. Compare both the prediction surface and final dots with the full registry. Exceeding the correlation or 3-pixel overlap thresholds is a duplicate stop, not a tuning target.

3 · exact-file validation

Reopen the bytes to be downloaded

Check band count, float32 type, CRS, shape, transform, bounds, 100 m resolution, footprint nulls, and finite `[0,1]` values. A local format pass is not organizer approval.
