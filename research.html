Hypothesis slate

Each proposal names an observable signature and at least one non-fault explanation. The target is a fault-confidence map; an inferred fault does not establish a geothermal vent.

Estimated DTI improvement is qualitative and untested: relative potential is highest for 01, medium for 02, medium-to-low for 03, and uncertain-to-low for 04. Costs are noted below. No numerical gain is defensible without a matched holdout.

01 · MODERATE COST · PROPOSED

Multi-scale signed edge offset

Layers & signature: isostatic gravity anomaly, reduced-to-pole magnetics and detrended elevation. Look for gravity/magnetic cross-strike edge offsets and signs that persist across smoothing scales, particularly beneath weak relief.

Discovery rationale: a buried juxtaposition could be absent from a surface-focused catalogue. Non-fault mimics: lithologic contacts, dikes, remanent magnetization and survey artifacts.

Difference & access: tests signed relative offsets against an unsigned-edge control, rather than a simple edge-magnitude product. Not implemented here; similar peer ideas exist and overall novelty is unknown. The owner-mirrored 19-band raster was restored, but the shared cached derived stack and an assigned lane are absent.

02 · HIGH COST · DATA CONTINGENT

Repeated drainage deflections

Layers & signature: detrended elevation, 3DEP terrain and 3DHP flowlines. Test repeated, similarly oriented stream deflections crossing an independent gravity-edge corridor against basin-matched controls.

Discovery rationale: multiple channel responses may expose a weak or buried structural line. Non-fault mimics: lava dams, erosion, roads and drainage capture.

Difference & access: repeated cross-stream deflection, not single-profile curvature or fluvial-χ. Official sources advertise downloads, but coverage, required licenses and binary retrieval are not verified locally. Not viable in this run.

03 · MODERATE–HIGH COST · DATA CONTINGENT

Matched shallow-temperature residual

Layers & signature: INGENIOUS 2-m probes, terrain and conductivity. Cross-fit local background temperature and test out-of-fold residual clusters against independently inferred geophysical edges.

Discovery rationale: anomalies may corroborate a concealed fluid pathway. Non-fault mimics: sun exposure, groundwater and uneven sampling. An anomaly is not a fault label.

Difference & access: models survey/terrain confounders rather than direct well proximity. GDR advertises a public CC BY dataset; target coverage and actual download were not checked locally. Not viable in this run.

04 · MODERATE–HIGH COST · DATA CONTINGENT

Acquisition-block radiometric residual

Layers & signature: GeoDAWN K/eTh/eU and flight-line metadata, with independent magnetic edges. Test whether an alteration residual is stable under held-out acquisition blocks.

Discovery rationale: persistent alteration patterns could support a concealed pathway. Non-fault mimics: rock type, soil moisture, vegetation and processing seams.

Difference & access: emphasizes acquisition-block invariance, not an unqualified ratio hotspot. Similar ratios occur in peer work; the official GeoDAWN release advertises data and CC0, but exact grids were not downloaded here. Not viable in this run.

How a future test earns confidence

  1. Pre-register the assigned lane, source hashes, comparable incumbent, complete registry and experiment budget.
  2. Hide whole fault segments with a spatial buffer; rebuild catalogue-dependent features and placement from visible faults only. Mask visible faults pixel-exactly.
  3. Test every feature alone for leakage; investigate AUC above 0.90. Report pooled HOLDOUT-DTI, evaluator version, withheld-positive count and a paired spatial-block 95% interval.
  4. Check both rank correlation and preview-dot overlap before placement, and again for final dots, against the complete registry. Missing or undefined comparisons fail closed.
  5. Validate exact TIFF bytes with the shared writer and an independent audit. A separate selector checks the weekly slot; no proxy guarantees a leaderboard score.

A shared-tool defect, not a private workaround

The pinned shared evaluator and writer now explicitly reject non-binary inputs rather than silently thresholding them; the official format also permits continuous values. Exact bytes are recorded in the provenance manifest. Unresolved upstream defects include fold buffer, zero-truth FP pooling, catalogue leakage diagnostic, surface overlap, and masked pixels. Until fixed in the shared template and independently retested, these tools do not establish a qualifying holdout or release. A separate saved-byte check is diagnostic, not a private evaluator fork or authorization.
