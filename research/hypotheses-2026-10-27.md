## Gating interpretation

1. These ideas are deliberately **not** presented as proven novel across the GEMSDOE family. Sibling pages describe many overlapping lines of work (thermal/geothermal proxies, DEM scarps/ridges, potential-field edges, and catalog-free patterns). A new name or changed rasterizer does not make a method unique.
2. Public source availability is not enough. Before a source is used, pin its download URL, checksum, license, metadata, projection, and footprint overlap. The GDR regional compilation is not the same file as DrivenData's official train/test assets.
3. A candidate advances only after the exact assigned lane is known, feature-only leakage canaries pass, every catalogue-based feature is regenerated fold-locally, visible faults are masked pixel-exactly, whole segments are withheld with a buffer, and pooled HOLDOUT-DTI beats the same-evaluator frozen incumbent with its required uncertainty interval.
4. The pre-placement score surface and final dots must both pass the literal complete-registry correlation and 3-pixel overlap stop. Do not move or thin dots merely to evade a registry collision.
5. Only then may a separate selector consider an authorized weekly slot. A planning rank, a HOLDOUT-DTI value, and a format receipt are each insufficient on their own.
