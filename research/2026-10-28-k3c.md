## 2. Why E2 (strain deficit) failed — interpretation

The prior is a ratio of regional geodetic second invariant to a smoothed catalogue slip proxy. Its single-feature
AUC (0.543) shows it carries almost no fault-scale information; multiplied into a surface that already encodes
the geodetic bands it mostly reweights budget toward low-catalogue-density basins where DTI truth density is also
low. The negative result is consistent with IR-K3-12 (catalogue-fragment bias): the hidden truth is spatially
correlated with the visible catalogue, so priors that push dots *away* from mapped structure lose more credit
than they gain. It should not be retried as a multiplicative prior; a possible future use is as a *tie-breaker*
inside the step-over band only.

## 4. Sensitivity the selector must know (IR-K3-21)

The verdict depends on the null domain. With p0 computed over the whole footprint the first pass flagged the
61GEMSDOE 311,617-dot `gravinversion-hgm k400k-b2zeros` raster at excess 0.83 (fail). That raster, like ours,
zeroes the 2-px catalogue collar, so the 4 % of the footprint it does not cover is exactly where neither file
can place dots; over the eligible domain its coverage is 99.37 % and the excess is 0. The eligible-domain null is
the correct one for a dot set constrained to that domain, but the switch was made *after* seeing the first-pass
result — that ordering is disclosed here and in the run card's `post_processing` field. 53 blanket/continuous
rasters are not identifiable by overlap (they cover ≥ 99.7 % of the eligible domain) and are judged by rank
correlation only (max |ρ| 0.527).
